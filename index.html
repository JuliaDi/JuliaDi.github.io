Julia Di

Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Columbia University

Hello! I am starting as an assistant professor in January 2027 at Columbia University. I earned my PhD from the Biomimetic & Dexterous Manipulation Laboratory at Stanford University, advised by Mark Cutkosky, and worked as an Sr. AI/Autonomy Researcher at Lockheed Martin Advanced Technology Center afterwards. During my PhD I was supported by a NASA Space Technology Research Fellowship, P.E.O. Scholarship, Zonta Amelia Earhart Fellowship, and the Future Space Leaders Foundation. I care about a great many things in life, including: robotic sensing, space exploration, art, climate change and conservation, and social impact.

Research
